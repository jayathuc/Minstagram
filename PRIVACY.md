# Privacy Policy

Minstagram is a digital wellbeing app that puts a pause between you and Instagram.

## What we collect

Nothing. Minstagram has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no servers. The app never connects to the internet on its own.

## What stays on your phone

- Your session history (intention, planned and actual time, reels watched) is stored in a local database on your device.
- App usage data, read through Android's Usage Access permission, is used only to know when Instagram is on screen. It is never stored beyond what the session history needs and never leaves the device.
- The accessibility service watches only Instagram's Reels screen, to count Reels and show a question between them. It does not read messages, passwords, or content from any other app, and it stores nothing but the reel count.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing does.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything.
